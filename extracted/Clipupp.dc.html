/ FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

How long does a project take?
It depends on scope. A small app or automation often ships in 2–6 weeks; a full product in a few months. You get a fixed timeline in the proposal — and we deliver on it.
How much does it cost?
Every project is scoped and priced to fit — no fixed packages. Start with a free consultation and you'll get a clear scope, timeline, and a fixed quote before you commit to anything.
Do we have to replace our existing systems?
No. We build around what you already use — Tally, Zoho, your CRM, ERP, or spreadsheets — and connect to them with APIs and integrations. Zero rip-and-replace.
Who owns the code we pay for?
You do — 100%. All source code, IP, and accounts are handed over to you on delivery. Nothing is locked to us.
What happens after launch?
We don't disappear. Choose a monthly retainer and we handle fixes, updates, monitoring, and new features as you grow — or take it fully in-house. Your call.
